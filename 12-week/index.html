Rules for every session

  1. No true maxes. Test days stop at a top set: the box squat at a top triple at RPE 8, the floor press at a top triple at RPE 7, the sumo deadlift at a top double at RPE 9. Estimate the max from it: e1RM = weight × (1 + (reps + reps in reserve) / 30) An RPE 8 triple is the weight × 1.167, an RPE 7 triple × 1.20, an RPE 9 double × 1.10. Every percentage uses the newest e1RM of that lift. The Thursday band bench uses the floor press e1RM. The calculator on the Lifts tab does the math.
  2. Squat loads at 85%. Box squat percentages are already scaled: 68 / 77 / 81% in place of 80 / 90 / 95%. The band box squat runs at 42.5 / 47 / 51% plus band, and its straight-weight sub at 64 / 68 / 72%. Rule 8 can lift this for weeks 7 to 12.
  3. Pressing stays at RPE 7. Two presses only, the Monday floor press and the Thursday speed bench. No singles, close or medium grip, shoulder blades pinned back on every rep. Rows stop when the elbows reach your sides.
  4. Joint check. Score any joint that's given you trouble from 0 to 10 during the session and the next morning, and log it at the bottom of each session. Stay at 3 or under during and 2 or under the next morning. Above 2 the next morning: repeat last week's loads for that area. At 6: drop that area's loaded work and cut the session to 75% of its volume. At 8: technique session at 60% intensity and half volume. Pain at rest with night pain, numbness or tingling, worsening despite rest, or feeling unwell: see a clinician before training.
  5. Box jumps are gated. They start in week 4 only if every lower-body morning score stayed at 2 or under in weeks 1 to 3. Use a box you land on in a quarter squat, then step down.
  6. Lighter accessories in weeks 4, 8 and 12. One fewer set on everything after the main lift.
  7. On-ramp in weeks 1 and 2. Week 1: half the written sets on every accessory, rounded up, and half the reps on the 100- and 200-rep sets. Week 2: one set fewer than written. Week 3 on: as written. Main lifts run as written from day 1.
  8. Earn-back in weeks 7 to 12. If every lower-body morning score stays at 1 or under through week 6, turn on earn-back in the Lifts tab. The box squat goes back to 80 / 90 / 95%, the band box squat to 50 / 55 / 60% plus band, the sub to 75%, a 2-foot depth drop takes slot B on Saturdays: 1 x 5 in week 7, then 1 x 10, from a 12 in box, and S-Drive sled marches replace the Wednesday Max Trainer sprints: 2 x 20 s in week 7, then 3 x 20 s, starting at a low sled level. Raise the box 2 in only after a week with every morning score at 1 or under. One morning score above 1 turns it off for the rest of the block. Pressing limits stay the same either way.

Cardio machines

Rower for easy work, Max Trainer for anything hard. On the rower, stop the slide before your shins pass vertical and let your heels lift at the catch. On the Max Trainer, hold the fixed handles and keep the whole foot on the pedal instead of driving up onto your toes. The S-Drive comes in only with earn-back, as a Wednesday sled march from week 7; pushing off the balls of the feet loads the heel, so it stays out of weeks 1 to 6.

Not in this plan

Couch stretch, deep squat holds, wall calf stretches, heel drops off a step, doorway pec stretches, sleeper and cross-body stretches, behind-the-back reaches, band pass-throughs and dead hangs.
