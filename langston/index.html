Test results

Day B of weeks 1, 4, 8 and 12. Inches and seconds; use decimals, like 68.5 for 68 1/2. Best try counts.

Reading the numbers

Small changes between two tests are often measurement noise: a different wall mark, a slow thumb on the timer. Look for the trend across weeks 1, 4, 8 and 12. Jumps should climb and sprint times should drop, but not every test will beat the last one, especially while he's growing fast.

Rules for every session

  1. Two days a week. At least one day apart, about 45 minutes each, never the day before a game. This replaces extra pickup or extra drills; it doesn't stack on top of them. If basketball was hard that day, do the warm-up, the lifts and the core work, and skip the sprints and jumps.
  2. Sprints and jumps are all-out with full rest. Walk back after every sprint and rest before the next one. Stop a set when he slows down or the landings get loud or sloppy. Tired reps don't make him faster.
  3. Lifts stop 2 or 3 reps short. Pick a weight he could do 2 or 3 more reps with. When every set feels that easy at the top of the rep range, go up to the next dumbbell or kettlebell. Clean form decides the weight, never the number on the bell.
  4. Landings. Quiet feet, knees over the toes and never caving in, hips back, chest up. On "stick" reps he holds the landing for 2 s. If the knees cave in, go back to the easier version until they don't.
  5. Pain rule. Answer the pain check at the end of every session. Any pain in the knees (especially below the kneecap), heels, shins or back means no sprints or jumps that day; finish the upper body and core only. Pain two sessions in a row, pain when walking, or pain that wakes him at night: stop and see a doctor or physical therapist before the next session. Those spots are common trouble areas during a growth spurt.
  6. Growth check. Measure height on every test day. If he grew 1/4 in or more since the last check, the growth flag turns on: depth jumps in weeks 9 to 12 become box jumps, and nothing gets added. Kids often look clumsy during fast growth; keep the reps clean and patient.
  7. Depth jumps are earned. They turn on in week 9 only if all 8 sessions in weeks 5 to 8 have a pain check of No and the growth flag is off. A pain check left blank counts against it. The app makes the swap automatically.
  8. An adult is there for every session, watching landings and lifts. Weeks 1 and 2 are for learning each move with light weights.
  9. Recovery. 8 to 10 hours of sleep a night. A real meal with protein within a couple of hours after training, and water through the day. No supplements needed.

Lighter weeks and tests

Weeks 4, 8 and 12 drop one set from everything. Day B of weeks 1, 4, 8 and 12 is test day: warm up, run the tests, then 2 sets of that phase's lifts. Log results in the Tests tab.

Not in this plan

Max-weight lifts, jumping down off boxes outside the depth jumps, high boxes, long jump circuits, distance running for conditioning, and Olympic lifts. Basketball already covers his conditioning.
